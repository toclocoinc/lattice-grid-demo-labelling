Label text in the browser, keyboard only

5,574 SMS messages. Give each one a label (ham, spam or unsure) from a validated list, then review what is left or where you disagree with the original label, and export a clean CSV. Your labels stay in this browser (localStorage) — nothing is uploaded.

Keys: arrows move, Enter opens the label list on the focused label cell, arrows choose, Enter commits; or type h / s / u then Enter, then ↓ for the next message. Tab or Shift+Tab leaves the grid for the next or previous control; Delete clears a label; Ctrl+Z undoes. “Disagreement” = your label differs from the original UCI label.
